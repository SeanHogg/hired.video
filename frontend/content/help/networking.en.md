Your network is your highest-leverage job-search channel. Build it deliberately.

- **Lead with value**, not asks — share work, comment thoughtfully, make introductions.
- **Follow up within 24 hours** of any meaningful conversation.
- **Keep a simple pipeline** — track who you've reached and when to circle back.
- **Use your video resume** as a warm, memorable intro when you connect.
