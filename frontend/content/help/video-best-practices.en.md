A great video resume is short, well-lit, and ends with a clear ask.

- **Keep it 1-3 minutes.** Lead with your strongest hook in the first 5 seconds.
- **Light your face** from the front and frame at eye level.
- **Clean audio beats clean video** — use a quiet room or a simple mic.
- **Tell one story**, highlight 2-3 key skills, and close with a call to action.
