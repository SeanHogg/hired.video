Set up your account and ship your first video resume in under 15 minutes.

1. **Complete your profile** — add your experience, skills, and a photo.
2. **Create a video resume** — upload a clip or generate one with the AI Studio.
3. **Set your visibility** — choose Public, Unlisted, or Private per artifact.
4. **Turn on job matching** — set preferences in Settings so relevant roles find you.

Want the full interactive walkthrough? Open the [Getting Started guide](/guides/getting-started).
