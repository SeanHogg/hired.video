# PRD: Job and article document-review adapters (#2254 remainder)

## Problem & Goal

Peer document review already runs on one engine (`resume-feedback`) with a `ReviewSubjectAdapter` contract (`load`, `applyEdit`, `validateDraft`, `save`) and polymorphic `subject_type` / `subject_id` columns that allow `resume`, `job`, and `article`. Only résumés are wired. `WIRED_REVIEW_SUBJECT_TYPES` is `["resume"]`, so creating or opening a job or article review is refused with `INVALID_STATE`. Create input still requires a `resumeId`. The review UI, invite dialog, and inbox are résumé-shaped, and edit apply/validate assumes résumé content.

Goal: make job and article first-class review subjects on the existing engine and reviewer-authoring flow, without a résumé foreign key, so owners can invite reviewers, collect annotations and proposed drafts, and accept edits on jobs and articles the same way they already do on résumés.

## Target users / ICP roles

- **Document owners**: hiring teammates who own a job posting, and authors who own an article, and need structured peer review before publish.
- **Reviewers**: invited coaches or teammates who annotate and propose drafts. They should not need a résumé id to open, comment on, or suggest edits for a job or article.
- **Inbox users**: people who receive review invites and need subject-accurate labels (job vs article vs résumé), not résumé-only copy.

## Scope

Wire the remaining #2254 adapters and the subject-neutral API and frontend that those adapters require. StudioLanding create-flow (#2221) and reviewer authoring UI (#2220), except the route and copy generalization below, stay as shipped.

In scope:

- Job and article `ReviewSubjectAdapter` implementations registered in `createReviewSubjectAdapters`, and both types added to `WIRED_REVIEW_SUBJECT_TYPES`.
- `CreateReviewInputSchema` generalized from résumé-only `resumeId` to `subjectType` + `subjectId`, with create/open going through the matching adapter.
- Subject-neutral frontend: `/review/:id` as the canonical review route, redirect from `/resume-review/:id`, `/r/:token` unchanged; invite, review page, and inbox hooks/labels no longer assume a résumé.
- Non-résumé `applyEdit` and `validateDraft` so proposed edits validate and apply against job and article drafts, not résumé-shaped content.
- Tests in `review-subject.test.ts` covering job and article adapters (load, create/open not refused, apply/validate/save).

## Functional requirements

1. **Adapter registration.** `createReviewSubjectAdapters` returns working adapters for `job` and `article` alongside `resume`. Each implements `load`, `applyEdit`, `validateDraft`, and `save` against that subject’s document, not a résumé record.
2. **Wired types.** `WIRED_REVIEW_SUBJECT_TYPES` includes `resume`, `job`, and `article`. Creating or opening a review whose `subject_type` is `job` or `article` loads through that adapter and does not return `INVALID_STATE` for an unwired type.
3. **Create input.** `CreateReviewInputSchema` accepts `subjectType` (`resume` | `job` | `article`) and `subjectId`. Résumé-only `resumeId` is not required for job or article reviews. Invalid or unknown subject types are rejected. Missing or unauthorized subjects fail closed without writing a review.
4. **Load.** Opening a job or article review resolves the subject by `subject_type` + `subject_id`, returns the current draft through `load`, and does not query or require a résumé foreign key.
5. **Edit apply and validate.** `applyEdit` and `validateDraft` for job and article operate on that subject’s content shape. Résumé field assumptions in `content-edit.ts` do not run for non-résumé subjects. Invalid drafts are rejected with the existing validation error style; valid proposed drafts can be saved and later accepted by the owner through the shipped authoring flow (`suggestedEdit`, `AnnotationComposer`, `ProposeDraftSheet`).
6. **Invite.** `InviteReviewersDialog` invites reviewers for the current subject (`subjectType` + `subjectId`). It does not require `resumeId` when the subject is a job or article.
7. **Review page.** Canonical route is `/review/:id`. `/resume-review/:id` redirects to `/review/:id`. Share links stay on `/r/:token`. Page and data hooks (`ResumeReviewPage`, `useResumeReview`, and successors) load any wired subject and do not assume résumé content.
8. **Inbox.** `useMyReviews` (and inbox labels) show subject-accurate titles/types for job, article, and résumé reviews and remain usable when no résumé id is present.
9. **Owner accept.** Accepting a suggested edit on a job or article persists through that subject’s `save` and does not write résumé tables.
10. **Tests.** `review-subject.test.ts` covers job and article adapters: wired (not refused), load, validateDraft, applyEdit, and save. Existing résumé adapter tests keep passing.

## Acceptance criteria

- Creating or opening a job review loads through the job adapter and is not refused as unwired.
- Creating or opening an article review loads through the article adapter and is not refused as unwired.
- Inbox, invite, annotation, and propose-draft flows complete for a job and for an article with no résumé foreign key.
- `/review/:id` opens the review. `/resume-review/:id` redirects to `/review/:id`. `/r/:token` still resolves.
- `review-subject.test.ts` includes job and article adapter coverage and those tests pass with existing résumé cases.
- `WIRED_REVIEW_SUBJECT_TYPES` is exactly the three CHECK-allowed types already stored: `resume`, `job`, `article`.
